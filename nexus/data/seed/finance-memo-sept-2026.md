# Finance Memo — Updated Travel Allowances (Sept 2026)

From: Maria Chen, Finance

Effective October 1, 2026, the daily meal allowance for business travel is increased to $75 per day to reflect inflation in Tier 1 cities. Hotel caps are unchanged.

We are also moving from Expensify to Ramp for all expense submissions starting November 1, 2026. Training sessions will be held in the last week of October.
