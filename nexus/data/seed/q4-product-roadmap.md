# Q4 2026 Product Roadmap — Acme Robotics

Owner: Product — Priya Raman (VP Product)

## Theme: Fleet Intelligence
Our Q4 goal is to launch Fleet Insights, an analytics dashboard that lets warehouse customers monitor robot uptime, battery health and pick rates in real time.

## Milestones
- Oct 31: Private beta with 5 design partners (Globex, Initech, Umbrella, Stark Logistics, Wayne Freight)
- Nov 21: Public beta, pricing announced
- Dec 12: General availability

## Key risks
The battery telemetry pipeline depends on firmware v4.2, which is currently two weeks behind schedule. Data residency requirements for EU customers are not yet resolved.

## Success metrics
Target 40% of active fleets using Fleet Insights weekly by end of Q1 2027, and reduce support tickets about robot downtime by 25%.
