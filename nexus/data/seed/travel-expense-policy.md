# Travel & Expense Policy (v2.1, Jan 2026)

Owner: Finance — Maria Chen

## Meals
Employees travelling for business may expense meals up to $50 per day. Alcohol is not reimbursable unless pre-approved by a VP for a client event.

## Flights
Economy class is required for flights under 6 hours. Premium economy is allowed for flights longer than 6 hours. Flights must be booked through Navan at least 14 days in advance where possible.

## Hotels
Hotel stays are capped at $220 per night in Tier 1 cities (New York, San Francisco, London) and $160 per night elsewhere.

## Submitting expenses
Submit all receipts in Expensify within 30 days of the trip. Expenses over $500 require manager approval. Late submissions after 60 days will not be reimbursed.
