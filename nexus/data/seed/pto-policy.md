# Paid Time Off Policy

Owner: People Ops — Sarah Lindqvist

Full-time employees receive 22 days of paid time off per calendar year, plus 10 company holidays. Up to 5 unused days can be carried over into the next year; carried-over days expire on March 31.

PTO requests of 3 days or more must be submitted in Workday at least 2 weeks in advance and approved by your manager. Sick leave is separate and unlimited, but more than 3 consecutive sick days require a doctor's note.

Parental leave is 16 weeks fully paid for all parents, available within the first year after birth or adoption.
