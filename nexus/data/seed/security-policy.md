# Information Security Policy

Owner: Security — Tom Becker (CISO)

All employees must use Okta SSO with a hardware security key (YubiKey) for multi-factor authentication. SMS-based MFA is not permitted.

Customer data must never be copied to personal devices or uploaded to unapproved AI tools. Approved AI tools are listed on the Security Confluence page and reviewed quarterly.

Security incidents must be reported in the #sec-incidents Slack channel within 1 hour of discovery. Laptops must have full-disk encryption and auto-lock after 5 minutes of inactivity.
